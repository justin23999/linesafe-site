LineSafe helps surveyors, draftsmen and field professionals record pipeline survey data, calculate levels, generate longitudinal profiles and create engineering deliverables.

It is an engineering utility for field-level recording and drafting: enter your readings, check the calculated levels, shape the profile, and — with LineSafe Full — export it to Excel, PDF or AutoCAD DXF.

Workflow

From survey data to engineering drawing

One project carries your readings, the calculated levels and the profile drawing, so the deliverable always reflects the data it came from.

  1. Survey Data Enter chainages, Known RL and BS / IS / FS readings, or import them from Excel.
  2. Calculations HI and RL are reduced from your readings, and Pipe Crown is calculated from Ground Level and Pipe Cover.
  3. Longitudinal Profile Review and edit the profile, add obstacles, fittings, references and annotations.
  4. Excel / PDF / DXF Export a workbook, a PDF profile sheet or an AutoCAD DXF drawing.
Who it is for

Built for the people who survey and draw pipelines

LineSafe is intended for surveyors, civil draftsmen, pipeline draftsmen, site supervisors and field engineers who need a dependable way to record levels and turn them into a profile.

Your data

Projects stay on your device

LineSafe works offline. Projects are saved locally on your device, and there is no LineSafe account to create. Files leave the app only when you choose to export them.
