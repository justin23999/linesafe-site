Workflow

From survey data to engineering drawing

One project carries your readings, the calculated levels and the profile drawing, so the deliverable always reflects the data it came from.

  1. 01
    Survey Data Enter chainages, Known RL and BS / IS / FS readings, or import them from Excel.
  2. 02
    Calculations HI and RL are reduced from your readings, and Pipe Crown is calculated from Ground Level and Pipe Cover.
  3. 03
    Longitudinal Profile Review and edit the profile, add obstacles, fittings, references and annotations.
  4. 04
    Excel / PDF / DXF Export a workbook, a PDF profile sheet or an AutoCAD DXF drawing.
